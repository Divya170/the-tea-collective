Our tea philosophy

Four small ideas.

The thinking behind how we present tea and welcome visitors.

Client to confirmWording to be approved by The Tea Collective.

  1. 01

    Appreciate the Ritual

    Warming the cup, measuring the leaves, waiting while it steeps. The ritual is part of the pleasure.

  2. 02

    Discover New Flavours

    From delicate whites to robust blacks, there is always another tea to get to know.

  3. 03

    Enjoy the Simple Moments

    A good cup doesn’t need an occasion. It only asks for a few quiet minutes.

  4. 04

    Find Your Perfect Cup

    Everyone’s favourite is different. We’re happy to help you find yours.

Our approach to tea

Thoughtfully presented.

A calm, unhurried space to look, ask and choose.

  • 01

    Presented with care

    Each tea is displayed and labelled so it’s easy to see, compare and choose.

  • 02

    Guidance when you want it

    Ask about any tea and how to brew it. There’s no pressure to buy.

  • 03

    Something for every moment

    Teas for bright mornings, slow afternoons and quiet evenings.
